# Licence of everything in this folder (src/data/own/)

The files in this folder are Grind Strat's own work, under the game's own terms. Ignas has not yet
chosen those terms. Until he does, they are the same as for the rest of the repository: all rights
reserved (package.json says `UNLICENSED`). When the terms are chosen, they apply to the game's code
and to this folder alike; update this file, the `grind-strat-own` record in
`tools/map/sources.json`, and `package.json`.

What is ours here: the names of rivers, lakes and seas over time (`names.json`: the forms read in the chronicles and other texts, each with its source named, and our rough outlines of two gulfs), the dated list of water works and other changes (the years, the facts in plain
English, which source says so, and what is still TO CHECK) and the shapes we drew ourselves, each
marked "reconstructed". The facts come from the sources named with each one (full details and
links in `data/sources.md`); a fact is not owned by anyone, but the selection and wording are ours.

Nothing from OpenStreetMap is in this folder: the dated OpenStreetMap rivers and lakes stay in
`src/data/odbl/` (ODbL 1.0), where they carry only our ids as tags. The shapes were drawn on
public-domain data (GEBCO heights and sea depths, Natural Earth land) and on published coordinates,
never traced from OpenStreetMap or from any map still in copyright.
